# Native ghost validation

Updated 2026-11-19. A qualifying identity control records the script's default
prediction before submission, then compares it with a fresh native job result.
Unobserved perks, fixture failures, uncontrolled deaths and ambiguous
counter-controls are not credited as successful identifications. Intentional
solo kill controls retain the prediction and speed trace before checking the
native result; a death by itself earns no credit. Five successes are empirical
coverage of the recorded conditions, not proof of universal uniqueness.

Most earlier five-round series predate the latest shared confidence and
lifecycle guards. Those guards have separate regression and engine checks;
the table does not imply five fresh games on the latest build for every ghost.

support to the native Headless flag. The headless flag is available before
photography; neither a missing nor a false flag excludes Dullahan. Vex is
excluded only by an explicit false InvisibleOnLIDAR value, including after

range gap invalidates the negative. The fixed-speed-2 stun and approximately
three-second recovery still provide positive support.
